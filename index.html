about us

SELVAS is a design studio where culture, art, and brands collide. We build vibrant visual universes for artists on stage and for brands in the wild. Rooted in the dynamic energy of Brazil's creative scene, we blend bold aesthetics with strategic thinking to craft identities that don't just communicate—they perform.

Founded in 2010 by designer Fabio Alves, SELVAS was born from the intersection of music, technology, and visual art. Our foundation isn't in traditional ad agencies, but in concert halls, festival grounds, and collaborative art spaces. This unique perspective allows us to bring a raw authenticity and a performer's mindset to every project, whether it's shaping the visual identity for an international brand or creating an immersive stage experience for an orchestra. We bring a perfectionist's eye and an artist's soul to every collaboration.
